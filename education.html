Curriculum vitae

Education and positions

Mechanical engineering, from production tooling and undergraduate design work through a doctorate in experimental contact mechanics and a postdoc building the test platforms.

Download full CV (PDF)
  • 2025 — present

    Postdoctoral Associate · Non-Tenure Track Research Faculty

    Center for Vehicle Systems & Safety, Virginia Tech

    Led the expansion of a single-wheel contact rig into a single-axle test platform: servo motor and drive specification, motion axis integration into the existing control architecture, safety circuit design, and custom instrumentation calibration. Running dynamic conformal contact testing under mixed creepage, and non-contact wheel defect diagnostics with MxV Rail.

  • 2022 — 2025

    PhD, Mechanical Engineering

    Virginia Tech · Advisor: Dr. Mehdi Ahmadian · GPA 3.91/4.0

    Dissertation: Effect of Wheel and Rail Conditions on Contact Dynamics and Mechanics. Experimental characterisation of non-Hertzian conformal and two-point contact, third-body layer effects on traction, and wear optimisation validated against baseline testing. Delivered to the Federal Railroad Administration.

  • 2020 — 2022

    Undergraduate Research Assistant

    Vellore Institute of Technology · Advisor: Dr. K. Anandavel

    Finite element contact modelling of stepped and back-to-back bolted joints in ANSYS APDL, with parametric evaluation of stress and strain at frictional interfaces. Best Paper award at a peer-reviewed conference.

  • 2018 — 2022

    BTech, Mechanical Engineering

    Vellore Institute of Technology, India · GPA 8.56/10

    Senior project: systematic process improvement in tooling on the 3C machining lines at Toyota Engines India, applying the Toyota Production System. Coursework and projects in additive manufacturing, machining and metrology, and lean enterprise.

Technical skills

Methods and tools

Weighted toward experimental design and measurement, with the modelling needed to interpret it.

Test systems

Rig design and commissioning, servo motor and drive specification, multi-axis motion integration, control architecture extension, machine safety protocols (STO, E-Stop, RTO), mechanical fixturing.

Instrumentation and DAQ

Custom PCB validation for wheel instrumentation, load cells, air-coupled ultrasonic sensors, pressure-sensitive films, signal conditioning, sensor placement, and calibration for signal integrity under operational loads.

Test and measurement

Design of experiments in JMP and Minitab across multiple degrees of freedom, test protocol development, instrumented impact testing, controlled laboratory and field campaigns, statistically rigorous technical reporting to federal stakeholders.

Contact mechanics and tribology

Hertzian and non-Hertzian contact, conformal and two-point contact, friction and adhesion, third-body layers, wear modelling on modified Archard formulations, rolling contact fatigue.

Analysis and modelling

Finite element analysis, multibody dynamics, constrained numerical optimisation including sequential quadratic programming, signal processing, model validation against experimental data.

Software

MATLAB, Python, JMP, Minitab, ANSYS (Mechanical and APDL), Abaqus, CONTACT, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, AutoCAD.

Programme delivery

Federally sponsored project management, scope and milestone ownership, sponsor reporting to the Federal Railroad Administration, proposal development, mentoring junior researchers.
